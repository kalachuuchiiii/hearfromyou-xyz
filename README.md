### Digital Cassette Tape Archive

Hearfromyou is an online space for creating and discovering digital mixtapes. Anyone can:

- Browse a growing archive of digital cassette tapes from people around the world
- Create a custom mixtape by selecting multiple song tracks
- Give the cassette a title and write a personal message
- Share the finished tape in a public archive for others to find and listen

The archive contains mixtapes made for someone special, unsent messages, memories, and quiet reflections. Visitors can search by name or nickname to see if a tape was left for them, or explore the collection like old cassette tapes found in an attic.

### Online Mixtape Maker Inspired by Cassette Culture

Mixtapes were never just playlists. They were personal keepsakes — small time capsules of emotion, memory, and music.

Hearfromyou brings back the nostalgia of classic cassette tapes and mixtape culture in digital form. Instead of a standard music streaming interface, the site uses authentic cassette tape designs, chrome and ferric labels, and animated spools to recreate the feeling of holding a real tape.

It sits somewhere between an anonymous message board and a digital mixtape collection — a quiet corner of the internet for sharing music and words as keepsakes.
