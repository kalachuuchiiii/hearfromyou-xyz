## What is Hearfromyou?

Hearfromyou is an archive of digital cassette tapes left by people around the world. Each tape pairs songs with a personal message. You choose the songs, write the message, and decide who it's for. That might be a person, a memory, or no one in particular.

It isn't a music service. It borrows songs from services that already exist and focuses on what they lack: the message, the recipient, and the object that holds them together.

In short: **a digital archive of mixtapes and unsent messages, for making personal keepsakes again.**

---

## Why it exists

Mixtapes were never just playlists. The order of the songs, the title on the label, and the note that came with it made them personal. A playlist is easy to make and easy to forget, while a tape has a title, a recipient, and a message.

Hearfromyou started as a **voice note** idea: record a message and leave it for someone to hear. But storing audio costs money, and the cost grows with every recording. So the project was reshaped around the cassette.

Instead of hosting audio, Hearfromyou lets people build a tape from songs that already exist, with audio from YouTube and iTunes. The site keeps only the tape: its title, tracklist, recipient, and written message. The constraint that ruled out voice notes gave the site its identity.

## How it works

Leaving a tape takes four steps on the submit page:

1. **Design your cassette.** Pick a title, a tape type, and a pink theme.
2. **Build a tracklist.** Choose real songs, with audio from YouTube and iTunes.
3. **Write a message.** Say what you'd want the listener to hear.
4. **Leave it.** Your tape joins the archive.

Listening is simpler: open the public archive, pick a tape, and press play.

## Anatomy of a tape

Every cassette on Hearfromyou is made of the same few parts:

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | The name written on the tape |
| **Tracklist** | Real songs chosen by the creator, with audio from YouTube and iTunes |
| **Message** | A personal note for the listener |
| **Recipient** | A person, a memory, or no one in particular |

Cassette editions have their own names, such as Homesick, Epilogue, and Longing.

## Features

**Create**

- Digital cassette tapes with different tape types and a pink theme
- Custom tracklists built from real songs
- A title and a personal message on every tape
- Choose who the tape is for, or leave it open

**Discover**

- A public archive of tapes left by people around the world
- Search by name to see if someone left a tape for you
- **Recurring Names** groups tapes and tracks by recipient, so you can follow every tape made for one name
- An individual page for every tape

**Keep**

- **My Archive** collects every track you've chosen and every tape you've left

**Feel**

- Analog-style cassette design with chrome and ferric labels
- Spinning spools while a tape plays

## Privacy and sharing

Every tape has a destination:

- **Sent to someone:** made for a specific person
- **Public archive:** left for anyone to find and listen to

The archive is about the tapes, not the people who made them. See the in-app [Privacy Policy](https://hearfromyou.xyz/privacy-policy) and [Terms of Use](https://hearfromyou.xyz/terms-of-use) for details.

---

## Design philosophy

Each cassette is built to look and feel like the real object. The interface is deliberately analog, not a generic playlist view, so opening a tape feels closer to picking something up than scrolling through a feed.

The idea is to slow things down: pick songs carefully, write something that matters, and leave it where someone might find it.

## Who it's for

- Anyone who wants to say something through songs and words, to someone specific or to no one at all
- People who enjoy listening to strangers' tapes and reading what they chose to say
- Anyone curious whether a tape has been left with their name on it

You don't need to be nostalgic for actual cassettes. The format is the frame, and what it holds is a message with music around it.
